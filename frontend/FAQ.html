Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about reserving rooms. Select a question to open it.

Open the Book a room page, choose a room, date, start time, and length, then enter your name and UH email. You will see a confirmation or a decline right after you submit.

Any UH student, faculty, or staff member with a UH email can book. A single booking can be 30 minutes to 3 hours, and you can book up to 60 days ahead.

Rooms can be reserved from 8:00 AM to 10:00 PM, every day. A booking has to end by 10:00 PM.

Your request is declined and the page lists the times that are already booked for that room. Choose a different time or another room and submit again. The booking page also shows each room's reserved times for the date you pick.

Study Pod 101 seats 4, Collab Studio 102 seats 8, Media Room 201 seats 6, Esports Lounge 202 seats 12, and Event Hall 301 seats 40. The form will not accept a group larger than the room.
